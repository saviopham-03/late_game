# 6. Task Management

The team will be using an **AGILE** approach to development.

The team will use:

- Kanban Board via Trello

The Kanban board will be split into 4 TODO Lists, where each list is an agile sprint.

### Working and Assigning Tasks

Every ***pending*** task should include:

- Task details
- Additional information that may be relevant such as pre-reading, references or examples

When a team member ***picks up a task*** they will:

- Put their name on the task to indicate by whom it is being done
- Move the task to the 'In Progress' list.

When a team member ***finishes a task*** they will:

- Put the task in the 'To be Reviewed' section.
- Request other team members to review work.

When a team member is ***reviewing a task*** they will:

- Put their name on the task to indicate by whom it is being reviewed
- Put the task into 'In Review' list

Once a team member ***completes a review*** on a task they will:

- Put the task into the 'Done' list
- Clearly indicate who completed the task AND who reviewed the task.

Members should regularly update task progress upon request.

## Appendix B - Meeting Role Expectations

### Meeting Chair

As the meeting chair you will be expected to:
- Give an introduction prior to starting the meeting
- Clearly state your name and role, and the name of the minute taker.
- Clearly state who is in attendance and who is absent.
- For those absent, clearly indicate why they are absent, even if reason is unknown.
- Go through the agenda points incrementally
- Facilitate and coordinate team member input, ensuring everybody has a voice and is heard.
- Resolve conflicts
- Clearly indicate what items are to be actioned and by whom per agenda item.

### Minute Taker

As the minute taker you will be expected to:
- Take a recording of the meeting if it is online
- Take simple notes on what was being said per agenda item
- Ensure main talking points are timestamped
- Clearly indicate on the minutes page what action items are to be done, by whom and by when.
